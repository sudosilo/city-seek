City Seek changelog

2026-11-10, v2026.11.10. Created City Seek, a huge zoomable city that is built fresh each time, with hidden items to find. Version three added a city builder with see through interiors, sandboxes, and towers, plus a tower floor view and an x-ray zoom. Ten of every hidden item are spread across the city, and tray cards count the copies you have found. Added swinging outside doors, flying birds, a Bluebird hunt, tap reactions, word bubbles, and synth sound effects with a sound toggle. Added office props, sports fields, basketball courts, traffic cones, and eighteen new hidden items.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Added Music mode, chosen from the Mode switch in the menu. The city now plays as a music visualizer: load a song or use the built in test beat, and the scene scrolls past in time with the kicks, bass and hats, either as a top down flyover of the city or as a side view out of a train window across downtown, works and suburb stages. Bold and Muted looks, callouts that pop in on strong beats, a beat sensitivity slider, a speed slider, an option to see inside buildings, and a hunt while it plays option where hidden items ride on the beat and have to be tapped as they pass. REC saves a video of the scene with the sound, in Screen, Tall HD or Wide HD shape. The beat engine lives in its own file so other games can use it.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Music mode gained a Lanes view that works like a MIDI waterfall dressed as a street: kicks are buses and trucks, mid hits are cars, and hats are people walking in a line, each in its own lanes, so fast runs of hits stack up into a queue that crosses the action line one by one. The action line now sits in the middle of the screen with a burst for every hit, a lane glow, and a frame pulse on kicks. The city flyover is drawn fresh each frame, which removes the glitchy patches.
