City Seek changelog

2026-11-10, v2026.11.10. Created City Seek, a huge zoomable city that is built fresh each time, with hidden items to find. Version three added a city builder with see through interiors, sandboxes, and towers, plus a tower floor view and an x-ray zoom. Ten of every hidden item are spread across the city, and tray cards count the copies you have found. Added swinging outside doors, flying birds, a Bluebird hunt, tap reactions, word bubbles, and synth sound effects with a sound toggle. Added office props, sports fields, basketball courts, traffic cones, and eighteen new hidden items.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Added Music mode, chosen from the Mode switch in the menu. The city now plays as a music visualizer: load a song or use the built in test beat, and the scene scrolls past in time with the kicks, bass and hats, either as a top down flyover of the city or as a side view out of a train window across downtown, works and suburb stages. Bold and Muted looks, callouts that pop in on strong beats, a beat sensitivity slider, a speed slider, an option to see inside buildings, and a hunt while it plays option where hidden items ride on the beat and have to be tapped as they pass. REC saves a video of the scene with the sound, in Screen, Tall HD or Wide HD shape. The beat engine lives in its own file so other games can use it.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Music mode gained a Lanes view that works like a MIDI waterfall dressed as a street: kicks are buses and trucks, mid hits are cars, and hats are people walking in a line, each in its own lanes, so fast runs of hits stack up into a queue that crosses the action line one by one. The action line now sits in the middle of the screen with a burst for every hit, a lane glow, and a frame pulse on kicks. The city flyover is drawn fresh each frame, which removes the glitchy patches.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. In the Music mode top down view, every beat object now lands on ground that suits it, reading a map of the city: cars drive along roads and parking lots facing the way the street runs, trees grow only on grass and are sized to fit, umbrellas open on sidewalks and plazas, and people walk along sidewalks and park paths. Balloons are the only things that float over roofs.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Music mode got a more illustrated look. An infographic layer adds a title banner for each section, a key with numbered markers and live hit counts, a big running total, short text panels, and chunky arrows that pulse on the beat, with an Infographic labels switch to turn it off. Train window buildings are now drawn as small isometric blocks with hatched sides, rooftop water tanks, vents and antennas, and some offices are cut away to show floors, workers and a coiled water pipe. Pylons in the industrial stretch are strung together with sagging wires. The Lanes street has manhole covers, painted lane arrows, storm drains, street lamps and vehicle shadows.

2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Music mode got a Rain view where every note is a raindrop: big drops for kicks, medium for mid hits, drizzle for hats, each landing on the street exactly on its beat with a splash and ripple. Storm clouds drift, strong kicks flash lightning, the skyline windows glow, and people walk the sidewalk under umbrellas. With Hunt while it plays switched on, the Rain view becomes a jar game: the drops come in five colors, the jar names one color, and you tap those drops before they land to fill it. Wrong colors shake the jar, every full jar joins the shelf and asks for a new color, and the song ends with drops caught, drops lost and missed taps. A Rain over every view switch adds rain and hat splashes to the other views.
