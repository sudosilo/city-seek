How to play
The whole city is far too small to search from up high. Tap anywhere to dive in, then pinch, drag and use the zoom buttons to work the streets, rooftops, parks, plazas and parking lots.
Each card in the top tray shows exactly what you are hunting. Tap a card to see its description. Watch for look alikes: a blue cap without the yellow backpack, a silver crown, a grey cat, a balloon of the wrong color.
Once you are zoomed in close, tap an item to grab it. Wrong taps count as misses. A hint circles the area around one item and adds time.
