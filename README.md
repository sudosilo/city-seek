City Seek

City Seek is a hidden item game set in a huge city that is generated each time you play. It is for anyone who likes searching a busy scene.

You zoom into the city, look inside buildings and towers, and find the hidden items listed on a tray. There are easy, normal, and hard levels, a hint button, a time counter, and a city code you can load to replay a city. Birds fly overhead, doors swing, and tapping things gets a reaction. Music mode turns the city into a beat synced visualizer that can record videos.

Live at city-seek.vercel.app

Latest change. 2026-11-13, v2026.11.13. Added Music mode, chosen from the Mode switch in the menu. The city now plays as a music visualizer: load a song or use the built in test beat, and the scene scrolls past in time with the kicks, bass and hats, either as a top down flyover of the city or as a side view out of a train window across downtown, works and suburb stages. Bold and Muted looks, callouts that pop in on strong beats, a beat sensitivity slider, a speed slider, an option to see inside buildings, and a hunt while it plays option where hidden items ride on the beat and have to be tapped as they pass. REC saves a video of the scene with the sound, in Screen, Tall HD or Wide HD shape. The beat engine lives in its own file so other games can use it.
